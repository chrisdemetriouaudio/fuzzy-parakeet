Privacy notice

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This website (chrisdemetriou.com) is run by Chris Demetriou, a sole trader based in Greater Manchester, United Kingdom. This notice explains what personal data the site collects, why, and what happens to it.

There are no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of your browsing. The chat assistant is an AI; Chris reads every conversation and replies personally. The only third party that would set cookies is the embedded YouTube player, and it stays switched off until you allow it. See the cookie policy.

What is collected

Only what you type into a form on this site. The workflow audit form on the homepage asks for:

  • A description of the process, and the tools it runs on
  • How often it runs, and how many people touch it
  • Your name and work email address
  • Your organisation, if you choose to give one

The audio enquiry form on the creative page asks for your name, email address, organisation (optional), topic and message.

If you use the chat assistant:

  • What you type
  • When you typed it, and the page you started on
  • A scrambled, one-way version of your IP address, used only to limit how many messages one person can send in a day

If you ask for your written audit: your name, work email, organisation (optional), and that you agreed to us using them.

If you use your portal:

  • Your email address
  • Your passkey’s public key (your face or fingerprint never leaves your device)
  • When you sign in
  • If you accept a scope: the date and time, a scrambled IP address, and a fingerprint of the exact version you accepted

Nothing else is gathered. There’s no newsletter and no profiling. A portal account exists only if you ask for an audit.

About the AI assistant

The chat assistant is an AI: Meta’s Llama 3.3 model, run by Cloudflare Workers AI. It isn’t Chris, and it says so. Its only job is to help you describe one piece of work, so Chris can write your audit. It makes no decisions about you. Its summary is for Chris, who reads the full conversation. Your messages aren’t used to train AI models.

Why, and on what basis

The information is used solely to read and reply to your enquiry. The lawful basis is legitimate interest — responding to a message you chose to send.

  • The chat, its limits and its bot check: legitimate interest. That means understanding and answering your enquiry, and keeping the service working for everyone.
  • Your audit request and portal: your consent, given in the chat form, and steps you ask for before a possible contract (the scope and accepting it).

Who handles it

Enquiries are read by Chris Demetriou at hello@chrisdemetriou.com. Your submission is delivered by Web3Forms, a form-delivery service that passes the message to email and does not use it for anything else.

Cloudflare:

  • hosts the website (since 22 October 2026), and processes your IP address in order to serve it
  • runs the chat’s AI model
  • stores the chat and portal data, in Western Europe
  • runs the chat’s bot check (Turnstile)

Resend sends the emails: Chris’s notice of your audit request, and your portal link. It sends them from Amazon’s servers in Ireland.

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google and your IP address is visible to them in the process.

Cloudflare and Resend are US companies. They protect any transfer of your data outside the UK with the safeguards UK law requires.

If you choose to load the YouTube player, or follow a link to YouTube, Instagram or TikTok, those services collect data under their own privacy policies. Personal data is never sold.

Storage on your device

The site stores up to two values in your browser’s local storage: whether you allowed or declined third-party embeds, so it does not ask again on every visit, and any display settings you choose (theme, text size, font, spacing, contrast, motion). Neither contains an identifier, and neither leaves your device. Clearing your browser data removes them.

If you use the chat or your portal, two strictly necessary cookies are set:

  • Chat cookie (__Host-cd_chat): keeps your conversation together for 24 hours.
  • Portal cookie (__Host-cd_portal): keeps you signed in for 30 days.

Cloudflare’s bot check (Turnstile) runs only when you open the chat. Cloudflare says it isn’t used to track you. See the cookie policy.

How long it is kept

A form message is kept only as long as needed to respond and to hold a reasonable record of the correspondence, then deleted.

  • Chats that don’t become an audit request: after 30 days, contact details, introduced names and links to profiles are removed, and the scrambled IP address is deleted. The anonymised conversation is kept to improve the service. The automatic removal can miss a name mentioned in passing; email privacy@chrisdemetriou.com and the whole chat will be deleted.
  • Audit requests: your name, email and organisation are deleted 12 months after the last activity. If the work goes ahead, they’re kept as business records for 6 years after the work ends, as UK tax rules require.
  • Your portal: download or delete your data at any time, with Download my data and Delete my data.
  • Message counters (used for the daily limit): 2 days.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you may ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data held about you, and you may object to its processing. Write to the address below. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

From your portal you can download everything we hold about you, or delete it straight away. No decisions about you are made by automated means.
