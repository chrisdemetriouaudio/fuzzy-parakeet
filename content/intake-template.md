# Project intake: 10 minutes per project

Answer in your own words. Bullet points are fine, and "don't know" is a useful answer. Your answers become the "In my words" part of a case study, and nothing is published until you approve the page.

**Project:**
**Date:**

## The work
1. **What was the problem, in the words of the person who had it?**
2. **Who was it for, and what was your relationship?** Commissioned (paid), volunteer, your own project, a favour, employment? Can they be named?
3. **What exactly did *you* do?** And what did others do?

## The thinking
4. **What was hardest, and why?**
5. **Name one choice you made, and why** (for example: static site instead of WordPress, checking every 1 to 3 days instead of hourly).
6. **What would you do differently now?**

## What happened
7. **What changed afterwards?** Real numbers only, with where they came from: hours saved, costs before and after, errors caught, feedback.
8. **Can you quote anyone?** Give their exact words, name or role, and whether they agreed to be quoted.

## What can be shown
9. **Screenshots, recordings or documents you're allowed to publish.** Who owns them, and who gave permission?
10. **Anything that must stay private?**

---

**Private evidence** (emails, invoices, contracts) stays in your own storage. In the record, refer to it by a label, for example `private:reach-email-2026-03`. Never paste links to Google Drive, Dropbox or similar.
