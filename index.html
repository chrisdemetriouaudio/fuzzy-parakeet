An acquisition lands on your desk.

The board calls it an application upgrade. Here is what it actually is.

Enterprise appsDoes their data model fit ours, or is this a migration wearing an upgrade’s clothes?~6 wks
InfrastructureIs there capacity, and is it in a region we’re allowed to use?~4 wks
NetworkingSite-to-site links, firewall rules, source IPs. None of it is same-day.~3 wks
SecurityNew integrations, new attack surface, new approvals — and a review board that meets fortnightly.~4 wks
ChangeWhich CAB, and have we already missed the cut-off for this quarter?~2 wks
ReleaseWhich window — and what else is already booked into it?fixed

Typical lead times. Yours will differ — which is rather the point of asking early.

Nine questions. Every one of them has a lead time. Asked in month one, this is planning. Asked in month four, it’s a slipped go-live and a board conversation.

03 — Method

Four things sixteen years teaches you

01

Dependencies have lead times

A firewall change isn’t a task. It’s three weeks, a change record, and somebody else’s backlog. Treating it as a task is how go-live dates slip.

02

“Fine” is not a status

Ready means demonstrated. A tested tunnel. A verified rule. Data that has actually moved between two systems, once, successfully.

03

The failure is in the gap

Six specialisms, six partial pictures, all of them accurate. What breaks is rarely inside one of them — it’s where two meet and nobody owns the join.

04

The cheapest question is the early one

The same question costs an hour in a planning workshop or a weekend at cutover. It’s the same question. Only the invoice changes.

05 — The work

Five programmes, and what each one turned on

E2open

Enterprise Cloud Transformation

Detail More than 80 enterprise applications out of an Equinix colocation facility and into Oracle Cloud Infrastructure.

Context

A SaaS estate serving more than 3,000 global organisations, running in a colocation facility with the operating cost that implies.

What made it hard

Eighty-plus applications, each with its own infrastructure, networking, database and integration dependencies — and no tolerance for extended downtime on any of them.

What I did

Worked across infrastructure, networking, databases, DevOps and applications through planning and migration. Validated readiness, surfaced cross-team dependencies, and challenged assumptions before each cutover rather than during it.

Outcome

All applications migrated successfully, contributing approximately £500,000 in annual operating cost savings.

Sysco UK · Capgemini

SAP ERP Transition

Detail A business-critical ERP transition across three organisations, with operational continuity maintained throughout.

Context

An ERP transition delivered with Capgemini as delivery partner, spanning three organisations’ technical teams.

What made it hard

Three sets of change processes, three release calendars, and no single team holding the whole dependency picture.

What I did

Coordinated technical readiness across networking, infrastructure, database and application teams, and ran workshops spanning customer, delivery partner and internal specialists.

Outcome

VPN readiness, firewall changes, monitoring and end-to-end integration testing all validated before production switchover.

E2open

Security Modernisation

Detail More than 3,000 customer integrations moved off legacy HTTP and FTP onto HTTPS and SFTP.

Context

Thousands of enterprise integrations still exchanging production data over unencrypted legacy protocols.

What made it hard

Every one of them belonged to a customer with their own firewall, their own change process and their own idea of an acceptable window.

What I did

Worked with networking, application and customer engineering teams to validate firewall readiness, source IP configuration and production connectivity ahead of retiring each legacy service.

Outcome

Enterprise technical debt reduced and production security improved, with minimal customer disruption.

Heineken UK

Production Connectivity & Operational Readiness

Detail Secure production connectivity established between head office and a new cloud-hosted enterprise platform.

Context

A new cloud-hosted platform requiring verified, secure connectivity into an established corporate network.

What I did

Worked between customer infrastructure teams and internal networking specialists, coordinating VPN implementation and connectivity validation.

Outcome

End-to-end data exchange verified with application specialists, and monitoring and operational acceptance completed before service transition.

E2open

Major Incident Management

Detail Seven years on a 24/7 Priority 1 rota for Tier 1 enterprise customers.

Context

A standing 24/7 Priority 1 rota covering enterprise platforms in active production use worldwide.

What I did

Assembled specialists across infrastructure, networking, databases, applications and development mid-incident; drove the technical investigation and tested the assumptions being made under pressure.

Outcome

Faster root cause identification, and technically informed updates issued throughout so engineering could stay on restoration.
