Biography

Chris Demetriou is a producer, engineer and musician based in Manchester. His studio credits span feature film soundtrack, commercial record and session work, with performance, arrangement and engineering roles on releases through DreamWorks, Warner Bros, Sony Pictures, Working Title and Jerry Bruckheimer Films.

Trained across two decades in professional studio and live environments, he has worked in rooms shaped by Hans Zimmer and Trevor Horn, and on sessions at SARM Studios. Live work runs from tour support through festival and venue shows to corporate engagements.

Alongside that work he runs an online worship ministry, publishing worship covers across YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.

Media

Alongside studio and live work, Chris produces radio imaging, sonic branding and podcasts: station imaging and interview editing for national broadcast, full audio identity packages for podcasts, and audio and video podcast production from edit to platform-ready delivery.

ON AIR is commissioned broadcast work. Everything else is self-initiated: written to brief, produced to the same standard.
