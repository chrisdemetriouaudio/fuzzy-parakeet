About

I’m Chris, a web developer in Manchester, UK. I build web apps that take the repetitive work off small businesses and charities, from online booking and payments to customer portals and live dashboards. You deal with me from start to finish, and I explain everything in plain English.

I came to this after 20 years in music, media and global IT. Studios taught me to listen first and get the detail right. Sixteen years on enterprise systems used by thousands of companies worldwide taught me to test everything before it goes live, and to keep it running when it matters. I bring both to every project, and build apps that are simple to use and cheap to run. I still make music in my spare time: see my creative work.

Good to know

What does it cost?
It depends on what you need, so you’ll get a clear price before I build anything. Running costs stay low: several of my apps run on free hosting.
Do I need to be technical?
No. Tell me the problem in your own words, and I’ll explain every step in plain English.
Is our data safe?
Apps use encrypted connections and secure sign-in, and collect only what they need. Before we start, I’ll tell you where your data is stored.
What if you’re not around?
Every project comes with plain-English notes on how it works and how to run it, so nothing lives only in my head.
