Photography Direction

Purpose

Photography should reinforce credibility, authenticity and professionalism.

The photography should feel editorial rather than corporate.

Every image should support the story rather than simply decorate the page.

⸻

Brand Personality

Photography should communicate:

* Experience
* Confidence
* Curiosity
* Calmness
* Technical credibility
* Approachability
* Thoughtfulness

Not:

* Ego
* Salesmanship
* Corporate clichés

⸻

Overall Style

Think:

* Apple editorial
* Linear
* Monocle magazine
* Wired magazine
* Financial Times Weekend
* High-end technology documentary

Avoid obvious “business portrait” photography.

⸻

Colour Treatment

Natural colours.

Slightly desaturated.

Premium.

Soft contrast.

Natural skin tones.

No excessive HDR.

No Instagram filters.

⸻

Lighting

Soft natural light wherever possible.

Large window light.

Cloudy daylight.

Studio lighting should mimic natural daylight.

Avoid harsh flash.

Avoid dramatic coloured lighting.

⸻

Expression

Natural.

Relaxed.

Interested.

Thoughtful.

Occasional smile.

Avoid exaggerated enthusiasm.

⸻

Clothing

Technology

Examples:

* Premium knitwear
* Oxford shirt
* Polo shirt
* Plain t-shirt with overshirt
* Smart casual jacket

Avoid:

* Suit and tie
* Loud patterns
* Corporate uniforms
* Large logos

Creative Media

Examples:

* Playing keyboard
* Recording violin
* Editing audio
* Mixing podcasts

Clothing should remain consistent with the technology brand.

⸻

Locations

Preferred

* Modern office
* Contemporary workspace
* Recording studio
* Home office
* Coffee shop
* Conference environment
* Technology workspace

Avoid

* White studio background
* Fake boardroom
* Generic handshake photos
* Staged meetings

⸻

Hero Portrait

One signature portrait.

Requirements:

* Looking slightly away from camera
* Dark background
* Calm expression
* Waist-up composition
* Plenty of negative space
* Editorial feel

This image becomes the primary portrait across the website.

⸻

Supporting Photography

Examples include:

Working at a desk

Reviewing architecture diagrams

Whiteboarding ideas

Meeting with colleagues

Recording music

Playing keyboard

Playing violin

Podcast production

Speaking at events

These images should feel observed rather than posed.

⸻

Technology Photography

Potential scenes:

Laptop displaying enterprise diagrams.

Notebook with architecture sketches.

Reviewing technical documentation.

Standing in a server room.

Conference attendance.

Collaboration with colleagues.

Working through dependencies.

⸻

Creative Media Photography

Potential scenes:

Recording session.

Studio environment.

Live performance.

Editing podcast.

Video production.

Mixing audio.

Worship performance.

Professional rehearsal.

⸻

Composition

Prioritise:

Negative space.

Simple backgrounds.

Natural framing.

Strong leading lines.

Shallow depth of field where appropriate.

Avoid clutter.

⸻

Image Usage

Images should never overwhelm the copy.

Photography supports the narrative.

The visitor should remember the ideas before remembering the photographs.

⸻

Image Placeholders

Until final photography is commissioned use descriptive placeholders.

Examples:

“HERO_PORTRAIT_EDITORIAL”

“TECH_COLLABORATION”

“WHITEBOARD_SESSION”

“PODCAST_PRODUCTION”

“KEYBOARD_PERFORMANCE”

“VIOLIN_RECORDING”

These placeholders should remain throughout development until final assets are available.
