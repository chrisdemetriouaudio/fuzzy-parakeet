Enterprise Diagram System

Purpose

Diagrams are a core part of the ChrisDemetriou.com experience.

They are not illustrations.

They are visual explanations of enterprise thinking.

Every diagram should simplify complexity and communicate relationships that would otherwise require paragraphs of explanation.

⸻

Design Principles

Every diagram should be:

* Purposeful
* Interactive where appropriate
* Easy to understand within five seconds
* Visually calm
* Consistent
* Accessible
* Mobile-friendly

Avoid decorative graphics.

Avoid generic “network node” illustrations.

⸻

Visual Style

Think:

* Enterprise architecture
* System design
* Product thinking
* Information design

Not:

* Infographics
* Marketing illustrations
* Clip art
* Generic IT graphics

⸻

* learning

* organisational complexity
* scale

⸻

Information Flow Component

This is the centrepiece of the homepage.

Technical teams appear on the left.

Business functions appear on the right.

Chris occupies the centre.

Information flows into Chris.

It pauses.

Context is added.

Dependencies are checked.

Relevant conversations are triggered.

Information leaves in forms appropriate for the receiving audience.

The technology remains unchanged.

The understanding changes.

⸻

Interaction

Hovering over nodes should reveal:

Typical conversations

Questions asked

Dependencies considered

Example scenarios

No pop-up walls of text.

Keep interactions concise.

⸻

Progressive Disclosure

Never display the entire enterprise at once.

Reveal complexity gradually.

The visitor should always feel in control.

⸻

Labels

Use language appropriate to the audience.

Technical examples:

* VLAN
* DNS
* API
* Firewall
* CAB
* Identity
* Monitoring

Business examples:

* Network preparation
* Governance approval
* Customer impact
* Operational readiness
* Recovery planning
* Service availability

The same event should appear differently depending on the audience.

⸻

Accessibility

Every diagram should:

Support keyboard navigation.

Provide descriptive alternative text.

Remain understandable without animation.

Offer reduced-motion alternatives.

Never rely on colour alone.

⸻

Responsive Behaviour

Desktop

Interactive.

Hover states.

Expanded relationships.

Tablet

Touch interactions.

Simplified labels.

Mobile

Linear storytelling.

Collapsible sections.

No loss of meaning.
