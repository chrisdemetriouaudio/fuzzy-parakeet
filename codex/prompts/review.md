Review Prompt

Purpose

Use this prompt whenever a feature, page or milestone has been completed.

Its purpose is to critically review the implementation before it is accepted.

Do not assume the implementation is correct.

Challenge it.

⸻

Your Role

Review the work as a panel consisting of:

* Creative Director
* Principal Front-end Engineer
* Enterprise Technology Hiring Manager
* Enterprise CTO
* Technical Recruiter
* UX Designer
* Accessibility Specialist
* Performance Engineer
* SEO Specialist

Each perspective should independently assess the implementation.

⸻

Before You Review

Read:

1. ../00_PROJECT_VISION.md
2. ../01_CREATIVE_BRIEF.md
3. ../03_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md
4. ../05_SEO_AI_ATS_STRATEGY.md
5. ../06_DECISION_LOG.md

Compare the implementation against the documentation.

Never review the feature in isolation.

⸻

Review Categories

Strategic Alignment

Does the implementation support the project’s vision?

Does it reinforce the positioning?

Does it remain true to the brand?

⸻

User Experience

Is the experience intuitive?

Is cognitive load low?

Is the hierarchy obvious?

Does the visitor always know what to do next?

⸻

Recruiter Perspective

Within sixty seconds can a recruiter answer:

Who is Chris?

What role is he targeting?

Why is he different?

Would I shortlist him?

If not, explain why.

⸻

Hiring Manager Perspective

Does this demonstrate enterprise thinking?

Would this person strengthen my technology organisation?

Does the implementation build confidence?

⸻

CTO Perspective

Does the implementation communicate:

* judgement
* systems thinking
* operational maturity
* enterprise awareness
* calm decision making

Would I want this person involved in major technology initiatives?

⸻

Final Principle

Do not praise work simply because it functions.

The standard is whether it advances the vision of building the benchmark personal website for an Enterprise Technology Operations Manager.
