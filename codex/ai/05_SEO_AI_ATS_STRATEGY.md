SEO, AI & ATS Strategy

Purpose

This document defines how ChrisDemetriou.com Version 2 should be structured so it is understood by:

* Human recruiters
* Hiring managers
* Enterprise technology leaders
* Search engines
* Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)
* AI assistants and AI-powered search engines

The goal is not simply to rank well.

The goal is to ensure Chris is accurately understood and recommended for the right opportunities.

⸻

Primary Objective

Every visitor, crawler and AI system should understand within seconds:

Chris Demetriou is an Enterprise Technology Operations Manager specialising in helping enterprise organisations successfully deliver complex technology change by identifying dependencies, reducing delivery risk and ensuring operational readiness.

This positioning should remain consistent throughout the website.

⸻

Primary Positioning

The primary professional identity is:

Enterprise Technology Operations Manager

This title should be reinforced naturally through headings, page titles, metadata and structured content.

Supporting concepts include:

* Enterprise Technology Operations
* Technology Operations
* Enterprise Technology Delivery
* Operational Readiness
* Technology Change
* Release Management
* Change Management
* Enterprise Applications
* Enterprise Systems
* Enterprise Integration
* Technical Operations
* Enterprise Governance
* Technology Transformation

Keywords should appear naturally within meaningful content.

Never write for algorithms at the expense of people.

⸻

Positioning Boundaries

The website should make clear that Chris is not primarily seeking roles such as:

* Project Manager
* Programme Manager
* Technical Program Manager
* Scrum Master
* PMO
* IT Support
* Help Desk
* Service Desk
* Customer Success
* Customer Support

Do not repeatedly list these titles.

Instead explain the distinction through the narrative.

Example:

Project Managers focus on delivery.

Chris focuses on ensuring the technology, dependencies, governance and operational readiness are aligned so delivery succeeds.

This is a positive distinction rather than a criticism.

⸻

Enterprise Value Proposition

The website should consistently reinforce that Chris adds value by:

* identifying dependencies
* challenging assumptions
* reducing delivery risk
* improving operational readiness
* recognising downstream impacts
* connecting technical disciplines
* engaging the right teams early
* supporting successful enterprise technology change
* simplifying organisational complexity

These concepts should recur naturally throughout the site.

⸻

Information Architecture

Every page should have one clear purpose.

Recommended structure:

* One H1
* Logical H2 hierarchy
* Descriptive H3 sections
* Semantic HTML
* Accessible navigation
* Internal linking

Avoid vague headings.

Prefer:

“Enterprise Thinking”

over:

“My Skills”

⸻

Metadata Strategy

Every page should include:

Unique title

Unique meta description

Canonical URL

Open Graph metadata

X Card metadata

Favicon

Structured metadata

Metadata should be written for humans first.

Examples should communicate expertise rather than keyword repetition.

⸻

Structured Data

Implement JSON-LD where appropriate.

Recommended schema:

Person

WebSite

WebPage

BreadcrumbList

Article

FAQPage (where relevant)

CreativeWork (Creative Media)

ProfilePage

Organisation references where appropriate.

Structured data should accurately represent the content.

Never use schema purely for ranking.

⸻

Semantic HTML

Use semantic elements throughout.

Examples:

header

main

nav

section

article

aside

footer

Meaningful landmarks improve:

* accessibility
* SEO
* AI understanding
* ATS parsing

⸻

Internal Linking

Internal links should reinforce the site’s narrative.

Examples:

Enterprise Technology

↓

Case Studies

↓

Articles

↓

Contact

Rather than isolated pages.

⸻

AI Optimisation

Modern discovery increasingly happens through AI assistants.

Assume services such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and Perplexity may summarise or recommend this website.

Write content that answers questions such as:

Who is Chris Demetriou?

What does he do?

What problems does he solve?

What types of organisations benefit from his experience?

Why is he different from a Project Manager?

Why would a CTO hire him?

Answer these questions clearly within the website.

Avoid ambiguity.

⸻

ATS Optimisation

Applicant Tracking Systems should correctly classify Chris.

Primary role:

Enterprise Technology Operations Manager

Supporting concepts:

* Enterprise Applications
* Change Management
* Release Management
* Operational Readiness
* Enterprise Technology
* Technical Operations
* Technology Governance
* Enterprise Systems
* Infrastructure
* Networking
* Cross-functional Technology

Avoid misleading classification.

Do not optimise for Project Manager roles.

⸻

Recruiter Optimisation

Recruiters often spend less than one minute reviewing a candidate.

Within that first minute they should understand:

Target role

Industry

Experience level

Unique value

Call to action

Reduce unnecessary reading.

Support scanning.

⸻

Hiring Manager Optimisation

Hiring managers should recognise:

Enterprise systems thinking

Technical credibility

Operational maturity

Calm decision making

Judgement

Technology leadership without executive exaggeration

The website should feel authentic rather than promotional.

⸻

Write naturally.

Use meaningful language.

Avoid:

keyword stuffing

hidden keywords

repetitive headings

artificial optimisation

Every page should answer a genuine question.

⸻

Success Criteria

The strategy succeeds when:

Recruiters immediately understand Chris’s positioning.

ATS systems correctly classify his role.

Search engines understand the site’s structure.

AI assistants accurately summarise Chris’s expertise.

Technology leaders recognise enterprise operational thinking.

The website attracts opportunities aligned with Enterprise Technology Operations rather than generic project management or IT support roles.

⸻

Final Principle

Optimise for understanding.

Not rankings.

The objective is not simply to appear in search results.

The objective is to ensure the right people understand Chris’s value quickly, accurately and consistently across human readers, AI systems and recruitment platforms.
