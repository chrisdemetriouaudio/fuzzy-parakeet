Decision Log

Purpose

This document records every significant strategic, UX, branding and engineering decision made during the development of ChrisDemetriou.com Version 2.

It exists to preserve the reasoning behind decisions.

Future contributors should understand why a decision was made before considering changing it.

If a future proposal conflicts with one of these decisions, challenge it thoughtfully and document the rationale for any change.

⸻

Decision 001

The website is a product, not a portfolio.

Decision

ChrisDemetriou.com should be designed as a premium digital product.

It should not resemble:

* an online CV
* a LinkedIn profile
* a traditional personal portfolio

Why

The goal is to demonstrate how Chris thinks, not simply where he has worked.

⸻

Decision 002

Enterprise Technology is the primary brand.

Decision

Enterprise Technology is the primary commercial objective.

Creative Media is a secondary professional discipline.

Technology receives approximately 70% of the visual emphasis.

Creative Media receives approximately 30%.

Why

Enterprise Technology is the career being actively pursued.

Creative Media strengthens the overall brand by demonstrating creativity, communication and technical craftsmanship.

⸻

Decision 003

The homepage begins with insight.

Decision

The homepage should not begin with:

* biography
* headshot
* job title
* list of skills

Instead it begins with:

Enterprise technology rarely fails because of the technology…

Why

Visitors should first recognise a problem before meeting the person who solves it.

⸻

Decision 004

Chris is introduced after the insight.

Decision

Chris’s portrait gradually appears after the visitor has engaged with the opening narrative.

Why

The story should come before the individual.

This creates curiosity and avoids another “Hi, I’m Chris” homepage.

⸻

Decision 005

Primary positioning.

Decision

Primary title:

Enterprise Technology Operations Manager

Why

It most accurately communicates the intended market.

It captures Chris’s operational, cross-functional and enterprise-wide thinking without implying ownership of project plans.

⸻

Decision 006

Differentiate alongside Project Managers.

Decision

Do not position Chris against Project Managers or Programme Managers.

Instead explain that the roles complement one another.

Why

Project Managers own delivery.

Chris helps ensure the technology is ready to be delivered.

This distinction is positive and more likely to resonate with enterprise hiring managers.

⸻

Decision 007

Enterprise systems thinking is the differentiator.

Decision

The website should repeatedly demonstrate:

* dependency awareness
* downstream thinking
* operational readiness
* enterprise coordination
* judgement

Why

These qualities differentiate Chris more effectively than generic competency statements.

⸻

Decision 008

Information Flow becomes the signature interaction.

Decision

The website should include a bespoke animated experience illustrating how technical information flows through Chris before reaching the appropriate audience.

Why

This demonstrates value rather than describing it.

It becomes a memorable explanation of Chris’s role.

⸻

Decision 009

The API analogy remains internal.

Decision

Do not describe Chris publicly as “an API.”

Instead use the concept to inspire interaction design.

Why

Technical audiences may appreciate the analogy, but recruiters and non-technical leaders may misinterpret it.

The animation communicates the concept without requiring technical knowledge.

⸻

Decision 010

Enterprise diagrams replace generic illustrations.

Decision

Use bespoke enterprise diagrams instead of decorative technology graphics.

Examples include:

* dependency maps
* information flow
* operational readiness
* release lifecycle
* enterprise ecosystems

Why

These visuals reinforce Chris’s expertise and create a distinctive visual identity.

⸻

Decision 011

Dark Mode is the intended brand.

Decision

Dark Mode is the primary visual experience.

Light Mode remains fully supported.

Why

Dark Mode aligns with the desired premium aesthetic and provides a comfortable reading experience.

Light Mode exists for user preference, not because it is the default.

⸻

Decision 012

Accessibility is part of the product.

Decision

Accessibility features should be integrated into the design from the beginning.

Features include:

* Focus Mode
* Read Aloud
* Reading Preferences
* Reduced Motion
* WCAG 2.2 AA compliance

Why

Accessibility improves usability for every visitor.

These features reflect thoughtful product design.

⸻

Decision 013

Design for scanning.

Decision

Every section should communicate one idea.

Avoid walls of text.

Why

Recruiters and hiring managers scan before they read.

Reducing cognitive load improves understanding.

⸻

Decision 014

Show more. Explain less.

Decision

Whenever possible replace text with:

* diagrams
* timelines
* relationship maps
* process flows
* animations

Why

Visual communication is faster and more memorable than lengthy explanations.

⸻

Decision 015

Premium SaaS inspiration.

Decision

Primary inspiration comes from:

* Apple
* Linear
* Vercel
* GitHub
* Raycast
* Notion

Why

These products share a disciplined approach to typography, spacing, motion and clarity.

⸻

Decision 016

Cookie consent should match the brand.

Decision

Implement a bespoke cookie consent experience.

Why

Trust and professionalism should extend to compliance.

The experience should feel integrated rather than third-party.

⸻

Decision 017

Mobile-first.

Decision

Design mobile first, desktop enhanced.

Why

Many recruiters will discover the website from LinkedIn or email on a mobile device.

⸻

Decision 018

AI-first discoverability.

Decision

Optimise for:

* Google
* Bing
* ATS
* ChatGPT
* Claude
* Gemini
* Perplexity
* Copilot

Why

Discovery increasingly happens through AI-assisted search as well as traditional search engines.

⸻

Decision 019

Position through examples, not buzzwords.

Decision

Avoid phrases such as:

* excellent communication
* stakeholder management
* strategic thinker
* cross-functional collaboration

Why

Demonstrate these qualities through stories, diagrams and examples.

Evidence is stronger than claims.

⸻

Decision 020

Professional photography.

Decision

Use editorial-style photography.

Avoid:

* folded arms
* exaggerated corporate smiles
* obvious stock imagery

Why

Authenticity supports credibility.

⸻

Decision 021

Build a reusable design system.

Decision

Every major UI element should become a reusable component.

Why

Consistency, scalability and maintainability.

⸻

Decision 022

Build documentation before implementation.

Decision

Create comprehensive documentation before writing code.

Why

The documentation captures the project’s philosophy and ensures future AI assistants understand the reasoning behind design decisions.

⸻

Decision 023

Git discipline.

Decision

Implementation occurs on feature branches with logical commits and milestone reviews.

Why

A clear Git history makes the project easier to review, maintain and evolve.

⸻

Decision 024

Preserve strategic consistency.

Decision

Future enhancements should strengthen the existing vision rather than redefine it.

Why

The project should evolve without losing its identity.

⸻

Final Principle

The value of this log is not in recording what changed.

It is in preserving why it changed.

Future contributors should understand the thinking behind the product before attempting to improve it.

⸻

Decision 025

Decision

Adopt an editorial systems visual language for the V2 homepage, with selective operational signals.

Context

The initial recruiter-first foundation established the right content hierarchy, but review found that the page relied too heavily on typography and read as a wireframe rather than a premium product.

Rationale

An editorial systems aesthetic gives the Enterprise Technology Operations proposition a distinctive visual form: bespoke dependency-map vectors, purposeful geometry, an editorial portrait treatment and restrained motion. Small diagnostic status cues may be used to make the operational perspective tangible, but the site must not become a dashboard.

Impact

The homepage visual system, portrait placeholder, information-flow diagram and visual motion treatment.

Status

* Active
