Customer: E2open

Enterprise cloud transformation

Migrating more than 80 enterprise applications from colocation into Oracle Cloud Infrastructure.

Explore case ↓

The objective

Move a SaaS application estate serving more than 3,000 global organisations from Equinix colocation into Oracle Cloud Infrastructure.

The dependencies

More than 80 applications brought infrastructure, networking, database and integration dependencies together, with no scope for extended downtime. Disaster-recovery planning and recovery testing were part of the readiness picture.

The contribution

Worked across infrastructure, networking, database, DevOps and application teams to establish readiness, surface dependencies and challenge assumptions before cutover.

Customer: SYSCO UK · Delivery partner: Capgemini

Enterprise SAP migration and business continuity

A business-critical SAP transition with operational continuity in view from the start.

Explore case ↓

The objective

Support a business-critical SAP ERP transition while maintaining operational continuity.

The dependencies

Networking, infrastructure, databases and application teams all had to be aligned. VPN readiness, firewall changes, monitoring and end-to-end integration testing were part of the production picture.

My thinking

Validate technical readiness early, bring customer specialists, Solution Architects and multidisciplinary engineering teams into the same conversation, and make assumptions visible before switchover.

The contribution

Facilitated technical workshops across multiple organisations and validated the readiness work needed to reduce implementation risk and strengthen operational readiness.

What it reinforced

Business continuity is not an end-stage check. It is the result of the right technical conversations happening early enough.

Customer: E2open

Integration security modernisation

Modernising more than 3,000 customer integrations from legacy HTTP and FTP to HTTPS and SFTP.

Explore case ↓

The objective

Retire unencrypted production exchanges and improve the security of a large customer-integration estate.

The dependencies

Customer firewall rules, source IP validation, connectivity testing and customer change windows all needed to align before legacy services could be withdrawn.

The contribution

Connected network, application and customer engineering teams to validate each migration path, reducing technical debt while keeping disruption to a minimum.
